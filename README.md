
# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things 🎬

## Introduction

My favorite series is **Stranger Things**. It is a *science fiction and horror* television series that combines mystery, friendship, and adventure.

> "Friends don't lie."

## About the Series

Stranger Things is about a group of friends who experience mysterious events in their hometown called Hawkins.

### Main Characters

- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers
- Max Mayfield
